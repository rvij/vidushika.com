Override Signals
conditions that change the entire plan
Accelerate — Deploy All Remaining
  • SPY drops 5%+ in one week. Don't wait for individual triggers. Every position gets better simultaneously — deploy all remaining tranches within 3 days.
  • Fed cuts rates at Oct 28 or Dec 10 meeting. O and STAG re-price before you can execute Phase 3 conditions. Buy immediately.
  • 10-yr falls below 4.50%. REIT tailwind is strong — deploy Phase 3 the same day.
Pause — Hold Cash
  • 10-yr spikes above 5.75%. REITs (O, STAG) and high-yield bonds (USHY) both reprice lower. Wait for stabilization — at least 2 weeks of flat or falling rates — before Phase 3.
  • Credit spreads widen sharply (HY spread widens 100+ bps in a week). Pause USHY and ARCC — high-yield stress is real. Redirect to SCHD or JEPI instead.
  • ARCC or MAIN earnings show NAV declining 5%+. Rotate the Phase 4 allocation to USHY rather than taking on deteriorating BDC credit.
Account Sequencing
put the right positions in the right accounts
IRA or Roth IRA — Priority
O STAG MAIN ARCC
REIT and BDC dividends are taxed as ordinary income at your marginal rate — not the lower 15–20% qualified dividend rate. Sheltering them in an IRA can save 10–20% of their gross yield in taxes annually.
Taxable Account — Fine Here
SCHD JEPI USHY
SCHD pays mostly qualified dividends. JEPI's option premium is ordinary income regardless of account type — no IRA benefit to offset it. USHY bond interest is ordinary income either way; the IRA benefit is smaller relative to the REIT names.
